## Subscribing

Choose Monthly or Yearly, then click **Upgrade to WJI VIP** or **Upgrade to WJI SVIP** and complete checkout. Your current plan's card shows **Current Subscription** instead of an upgrade button.

## Yearly credits arrive monthly

A yearly subscription does not deposit twelve months of credits at once. They are issued **month by month** across the year.

## Cancelling

From your dashboard, the membership card has a **Cancel subscription** button.

Cancelling stops the renewal, not the subscription:

- You keep your benefits **until the end of the period you have paid for**
- Credits already issued **remain valid**
- **No refund is issued** for the remainder of the period

## Changing plan

<Warning>
  **There is no upgrade or downgrade path.** While you have an active subscription, subscribing to a different plan is refused with a message saying you are already a member.

  To move between VIP and SVIP: cancel, wait for the current period to end, then subscribe to the plan you want.
</Warning>

This applies in both directions, and the button on the plan card will report the error rather than completing.
