## The plans

| | Free | VIP | SVIP |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly | — | $19.90 | $39.90 |
| Yearly | — | $199 | $399 |
| Credits per month | 300 free grant only | 4,500 | 10,000 |
| Models | A curated selection | All models | All models |
| Queue | Public queue | Priority | Priority |
| Concurrent generations | 1 | 4 | 8 |

VIP and SVIP also unlock virtual-presenter and HD video models.

<Note>
  You may see "3 and 5 concurrent tasks" quoted in the subscription benefits panel. The figures that
  actually apply are the ones above — 1, 4, and 8.
</Note>

Yearly billing works out around 16% cheaper per month.

## Subscribing

Go to **Subscribe** from the profile menu, choose monthly or yearly, and complete checkout.

## Yearly credits arrive monthly

A yearly subscription does not deposit twelve months of credits at once. They are issued **month by
month** across the year.

## Cancelling

From your dashboard, the membership card has a **Cancel subscription** button.

Cancelling stops the renewal, not the subscription:

- You keep your benefits **until the end of the period you have paid for**
- Credits already issued **remain valid**
- **No refund is issued** for the remainder of the period

## Changing plan

<Warning>
  **There is no upgrade or downgrade path.** While you have an active subscription, subscribing to a
  different plan is refused with a message saying you are already a member.

  To move between VIP and SVIP: cancel, wait for the current period to end, then subscribe to the
  plan you want.
</Warning>

This applies in both directions, and the button on the plan card will report the error rather than
completing.
