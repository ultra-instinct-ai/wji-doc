---
title: "Viewer access"
description: "Control what viewers can see and do, down to individual apps and canvases."
---

Viewers get read-only access by default, but you can narrow it further. Open **Settings → Members → Viewer permissions**. Only the workspace owner sees this panel.

It has four sections.

## Credit visibility

A single toggle. When it is off, viewers do not see the credit badge in the app bar or any spend figures. Turn it off when you are sharing work with a client and do not want costs on screen.

## Visible apps

Tick the apps viewers may open. Anything you untick disappears from their left panel.

<Note>
  If you hide the Infinite Canvas from viewers, they are told the app has not been added rather than seeing an empty panel.
</Note>

## Comments and likes

Two toggles controlling whether viewers can leave comments and likes on assets. Both are off unless you enable them.

The most granular section. You choose:

- **Which canvases** viewers can open, one by one
- **Which color tags** are visible — nodes carrying a hidden tag do not appear
- **Whether viewers may add annotations** — title, comment, task list, and arrow nodes

Color tags make this genuinely useful. Tag your work-in-progress nodes with one color, expose only the approved color to viewers, and a client sees a clean board while you keep working around them.

## What a viewer actually sees

When viewer restrictions apply, WJI strips the interface down:

| Area | What remains |
| --- | --- |
| Node toolbar | **Download** and **Asset preview** only |
| Multi-select toolbar | **Download selected** only |
| Canvas | Asset library, director bar, and settings are hidden; connection handles do not appear |
| Editing | Cannot paste, cannot move nodes, cannot create generation nodes |
| Annotations | Allowed only if you enabled them |
