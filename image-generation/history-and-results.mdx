Everything you generate appears in the history below the form, newest first. Scrolling loads more.

## Filtering

Two rows of controls:

| Row | Options |
| --- | --- |
| Scope | **All**, the current dataset's name, or **Bookmarked** |
| Media type | **All**, **Image**, **Video**, **Audio** |

A view toggle switches between list and grid. Audio only appears as an option when you are working in a dataset context.

## Actions on an entry

| Action | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Bookmark** | Adds it to the bookmarked filter |
| **Edit again** | Loads model, ratio, batch size, prompt, references, and model settings back into the form |
| **Rerun** | Restores the settings and submits immediately, skipping prompt optimization |
| **HD upscale** | Seedance results with a seed |
| **Move to current dataset** | Refiles the entry |
| **Delete** | Asks for confirmation |

Hovering an individual image or clip gives you more:

- **Download**
- **Describe** the image, video, or audio with AI
- **Edit again** — reconstructs the settings from the asset itself
- Add as **reference image**, **reference video**, or **reference audio**
- Set as **first frame** or **last frame**
- Inside a workspace: **Save to Idea**, **Set as Idea cover**, **Create idea from image**

You can also drag a result straight onto a canvas, or click it to open the full preview dialog.

## Retrying a failure

There is no retry button. Use **Rerun** on the history entry, or **Edit again** if you want to change something first.

Failures show a plain-language reason. See [Troubleshooting](/reference/troubleshooting) for what each one means.

## Credits

The credits figure on a history entry is the **actual** charge, not the estimate. For models billed per second or per minute it is filled in shortly after the result completes, once the real duration is known.
