WJI is a creative workspace for people who make visual stories. You collect your ideas, turn them into images and video with AI, and arrange the results into storyboards and edits — without leaving the same workspace.

## What you work with

Everything in WJI lives inside a **workspace**. A workspace holds your ideas, your generated media, and your canvases, and it bundles the apps you work in.

<Columns cols={3}>
  <Card title="Idea Studio" icon="lightbulb" href="/idea-studio/overview">
    Capture stories, characters, locations, props, and shots. Everything you write here can be reused by the other apps.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Idea Image Generation" icon="image" href="/image-generation/overview">
    Turn ideas into images, video, and audio. Reference your existing material so results stay consistent.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Infinite Canvas" icon="diagram-project" href="/infinite-canvas/overview">
    A node-based board where connections feed context into each generation. Build shot sequences and assemble them on a timeline.
  </Card>
</Columns>

A fourth app, [Digital Host](/digital-host/overview), turns a script into presenter-led video. It is not enabled by default.

## Who it is for

WJI is built for creators and the teams around them — people writing and shooting visual stories, and the managers who handle members, access, and spend. This documentation is written for both.
