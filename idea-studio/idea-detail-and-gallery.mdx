An idea opens in **detail view**. A button at the top right switches to **gallery view**.

The page is made of:

- **The idea card** — clicking its avatar jumps straight into Idea Image Generation with this idea
  selected
- **Detailed content** — rendered Markdown, with a copy button and an expand control
- **Images** — a grid of everything attached. Upload more, download or delete individual items,
  open the asset preview, and like an image
- **Image prompt**, **video prompt**, and **metadata** — collapsible panels, each with a copy button
- **Edit** and **Delete** at the bottom

Gallery view is a full-screen black canvas for looking at the images rather than the text.

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| `←` `→` | Previous and next image |
| `↑` `↓` | Previous and next idea |
| `Esc` | Back to detail view |

A thumbnail strip along the bottom jumps between images. The bottom right has asset preview and a
like button — hovering the like button shows who liked it and when.

## The asset preview dialog

Opening any image gives you a larger dialog with more to work with:

- **Basic info** — name, type, created and updated times
- **Asset details** — file size, resolution, aspect ratio, duration
- **Generation details** — model, seed, resolution, mode, aspect ratio, batch size, duration, and
  credits charged
- **Original and final prompts**, each with a copy button
- **Reference assets** used in the generation
- **Tags**
- **Use for generation again** — loads the original settings back into the generation form

The right side has tabs for **Info** and **Comments**, with a like button beside them. Renaming the
asset is done inline at the top.

<Note>
  Comments and likes are available only when the workspace allows them. See
  [Viewer access](/workspaces/viewer-access).
</Note>
